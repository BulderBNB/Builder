The chain itself

A quick history of BNB Chain

The mascot has a story. So does the chain he's built on.

2017

Binance is founded

Changpeng Zhao (CZ) and Yi He launch Binance. BNB debuts as an ERC-20 token on Ethereum to fund the exchange.

2019

Binance Chain goes live

BNB migrates off Ethereum onto its own native chain (BEP-2). Fast, built for trading — but no smart contracts yet.

2020

Binance Smart Chain launches

An EVM-compatible chain arrives alongside it, bringing smart contracts, DeFi, and — eventually — meme coins to the ecosystem.

2022

One chain, one name: BNB Chain

Binance Chain and Binance Smart Chain unify under a single identity — BNB Chain — as the ecosystem matures beyond its exchange roots.

2026

Leading where it counts

BNB Chain leads in RWA holders, tokenized stock market share, and real BNB paid out to meme holders through platforms like Flap. The chain that ships, still shipping.
