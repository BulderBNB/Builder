How it works

Five steps, nothing held in escrow.

Payment and chat happen off-platform — X, Telegram, a direct transfer. This is where the work gets posted, claimed, and remembered.

1

Post

Title, what you need, the reward, a deadline.

2

Claim

A builder claims it. Contact details swap off-platform from here.

3

Do the work

Wherever it naturally lives — GitHub, X, Figma, wherever.

4

Submit proof

A link to the finished work, not a file upload.

5

Approve & pay

You approve on-chain, you pay off-platform. Points bank either way.

The rules

What keeps this honest

⏱️

Miss a deadline, take a strike

Automatic, not a judgment call — the chain just checks the clock. Three strikes and a builder can't claim new bounties.

⚖️

Non-payment is reviewed, not automatic

We can't see money move off-platform, so a report is a claim, not proof. It's logged publicly, but blacklisting a poster is a human call, never a single accusation.

🏆

Top builders get highlighted

The best track records get featured on X — the real incentive here isn't just points, it's getting seen.
